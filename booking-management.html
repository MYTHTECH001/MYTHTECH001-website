The brief

the real thing.

A customer-facing booking form and a staff-facing operations surface have different responsibilities. The system needed to make requests visible to staff while protecting the boundary between preview work and live restaurant operations.

What was shaped

The build is deliberately isolated: separate Vercel and Supabase projects, an explicit PREVIEW_MODE requirement, a visible preview banner, and customer requests that begin as pending_call rather than becoming automatically confirmed. The staff surface supports authenticated reads and conflict-safe actions while keeping production untouched.
