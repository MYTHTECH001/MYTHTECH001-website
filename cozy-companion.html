Most productivity tools optimise for output. Cozy starts from a different question: how can software help someone move through a day with more intention and less pressure?

What was shaped

The product language is deliberately warm and spacious. The structure brings together a time-based greeting, daily progress, habits, a journey timeline, calendar views, journaling, mood reflection, and a non-judgemental AI companion. The design system uses warm cream, sage green, soft mint, and sand to make the interface feel restorative rather than demanding.
