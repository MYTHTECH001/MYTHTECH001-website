Case study / 02 · Web experience

A warmer way to find your place.

A mobile-first refresh for a multi-location Indian food and sweets brand, focused on clarity, confidence, and making local information useful.

Mobile-firstContent designLocations
SEHAJINDIAN FOOD & SWEETS
five locations

The brief

Start with
the real thing.

The source experience had important information spread across the brand: location names, addresses, hours, phone numbers, and existing image assets. The redesign needed to make that information easy to use without inventing missing menu prices or unsupported ordering destinations.

What was shaped

The refresh puts the brand introduction first, then gives location details a clear, repeatable rhythm. It treats mobile as a primary composition, keeps the content grounded in verified information, and leaves space for approved brand photography to strengthen the next iteration.

ScopeMobile-first website refresh
