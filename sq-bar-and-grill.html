A social dining experience, made clear.

A complete digital home for SQ Bar & Grill in Penrith — shaped around discovery, decision-making, and the moment a guest is ready to book.

The brief

Start with
the real thing.

SQ needed more than a brochure site. The experience had to help people understand the venue, browse the menu, plan a visit, discover group dining, and move into booking without friction.

What was shaped

The site was organised as a five-page experience: a confident landing page, a detailed menu explorer, an about page, a functions page for group dining, and a visit page for practical planning. Navigation was then made consistent across the full site, with page-aware states and a responsive mobile menu.
